投机解码训练 / Draft Model Training2026

DeepSpec-v5

面向 DeepSeek-V4-Flash 从零补齐 MoE 草稿模型的投机解码训练：将训练后端由 FSDP2 迁移至 Megatron-Core（专家并行、Grouped GEMM、FP8），并以两阶段 rollout 缓存重构特征流水线，在 8×H200 上把单步训练压缩约 5 倍。草稿算法基于 DSpark（半自回归 + 同策略蒸馏）。Built end-to-end speculative-decoding training for the DeepSeek-V4-Flash MoE draft model from scratch: migrated the training backend from FSDP2 to Megatron-Core (expert parallelism, Grouped GEMM, FP8) and rebuilt the feature pipeline with two-stage rollout caching, cutting per-step training time ~5× on 8×H200. Draft algorithm based on DSpark (semi-autoregressive with on-policy distillation).

实现过程 / Implementation

模型支持：针对原版主要支持 Qwen3、Gemma 等目标模型的情况，研究 DSpark/OPD、目标模型特征导出与训练框架，补充 DeepSeek-V4-Flash 支持；以 FSDP2 打通数据处理、target rollout、特征蒸馏与 checkpoint 流程。Model support: starting from upstream support for targets such as Qwen3 and Gemma, studied DSpark/OPD, target-model feature export and training frameworks, then added DeepSeek-V4-Flash support. Validated data processing, target rollouts, feature distillation and checkpointing with FSDP2.

MoE 训练：针对 MoE 模型的显存、专家计算与通信瓶颈，将模型和训练器迁移至 Megatron-Core，引入专家并行、Grouped GEMM、分布式优化器及 BF16/FP8 混合精度；通过权重转换与并行一致性测试，完成预训练草稿模型权重加载和多卡专家切分。MoE training: migrated the model and trainer to Megatron-Core to address memory, expert-compute and communication bottlenecks, adding expert parallelism, Grouped GEMM, a distributed optimizer and BF16/FP8 mixed precision. Weight conversion and parallel-consistency tests enabled pretrained DSpark weight loading and expert sharding across GPUs.

流水线优化：加入两阶段 rollout 缓存、异步特征预取、统一 FeatureClient、/dev/shm 共享内存传输、服务自愈与训练启动门控，形成 DeepSpec-v5。Pipeline optimization: added two-stage rollout caching, asynchronous feature prefetching, a unified FeatureClient, /dev/shm shared-memory transfer, automatic service recovery and training-start readiness checks to form DeepSpec-v5.
