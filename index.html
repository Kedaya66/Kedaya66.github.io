01 / 关于 · About

从视觉研究到大模型系统，让技术路径在真实场景中持续演进。From visual research to LLM systems, evolving through real-world problems.

在汕头大学工学院，我从计算机视觉和医学影像出发，围绕半监督学习、图像分割、数据效率、表征学习与模型泛化开展研究。At the College of Engineering, Shantou University, I worked on computer vision and medical imaging, including semi-supervised learning, image segmentation, data-efficient learning, representation learning and model generalization.

进入产业后，研究与工程实践依次延伸至多模态理解、语义检索与 RAG、强化学习、训练推理系统、投机解码、Agent 微调及 AI-native 应用。In industry, my work expanded into multimodal understanding, semantic retrieval and RAG, reinforcement learning, training and inference systems, speculative decoding, agent fine-tuning and AI-native applications.
